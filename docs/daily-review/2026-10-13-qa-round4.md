# 2026-10-13 · QA round 4, the full report and its files

The owner sent the round-4 report with its lesson-by-lesson CSV, the audio
CSV and a listening pack. Round 4 tested build `94b83d1`, the build before
the 13 October fix list. That list was drawn from this report, so its items
are answered in [the QA list record](2026-10-13-qa-list.md). This page
covers only what the files add, re-checked on today's build.

## Re-checked on today's build

- **H3 spellings the report still saw refused.**
  - Now accepted: kaisay, jootay, whale, x-ray and kurra-e-ardh exactly;
    reet and jehaiz as close.
  - Still refused, on purpose: zehen (ذہن, "mind", is another word from
    ذہین) and dozain (the English "dozen", not darjan).
  - Run through the app's own `matchesWord`, `nearMiss` and loanword list.
- **M8, asterisks in the grammar data.** They are there in the source, and
  `withEmphasis` turns them into emphasis wherever drill reasons, notes and
  summaries are shown. Reading the bundle cannot see that. No path lesson
  title or subtitle carries one.
- **L6, "dermiyaan".** Only the id. The word reads darmiyaan.
- **L14, tile labels.** Sentence tiles say "Word tile: …" and letter tiles
  name their letter.
- **M27, the trace pad label.** The 4 px overflow at 412 px did not
  reproduce: no element on l-1 or l-2 overflows at 277, 320, 360 or 412 px.
- **M27, "Got it" below the fold.** This morning's fix made it worse: it put
  the letter forms in a 2×2 grid on every phone under 380 px, so on a 360 px
  phone at normal text size, where four across fits, "Got it" fell another
  109 px. Fixed in `e7e68ec`: the grid now wraps only when four tiles cannot
  fit. It is still 193 px below the fold at 320×568 and 97 px at 360×740,
  queued as Q-063.
- **The 404 in the console of every lesson.** It is the page itself: GitHub
  Pages answers a lesson URL with 404 (M22). The same lessons served
  locally log no failed request.
- **"Every reading and dialogue lesson opens with the same due card."** A card
  woven into those lessons is graded through the same `gradeItem` as any
  other, so a right answer moves it out of the queue. The most likely cause
  is that the 29 re-runs started from one saved state. Not reproduced.
