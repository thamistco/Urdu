# 2026-10-23 · Tester, persona 1: a heritage speaker who cannot read

The first run of `qaaf-tester` (34 tool calls, about 15 minutes, read-only),
on tonight's build at 390x844 and 320x568. The persona grew up in
Birmingham speaking Urdu at home, understands it well spoken, cannot read
the script, and wants to read their grandmother's letters. It onboarded as
they would: read and write it, script first, the woman's voice, "I already
speak or understand it", four guessed placement questions, Steady.

## Where they would most likely give up

**At the start of lesson 2.** "Position practice" opens with "A NEW LETTER:
alif" and drills the same five letters as lesson 1 for 45 more steps. After
about 88 exercises they can name five letters and have read no word they
would recognise. For someone who came to read family letters, it feels like
being sent back to the start. Proposal P-119: a word they know, in script,
before the second letter lesson.

## Everything else, worst first

1. **High. Told they know the words, then told they know none** (Q-093).
   After placement, 50 word lessons read "Skipped as already known", but
   Review says "Read faster … opens once you know 10 words; you know 0". The
   feature built for exactly this learner stays locked. Sorted as before
   launch.
2. **Medium. The placement check has no "I can't read this"** (Q-094): four
   script-only questions with no sound, so they guess at پانی.
3. **Medium. Two right-looking tiles in "Which tile is be?" in کتاب**
   (Q-052, already queued before launch): اب and تاب both hold be.
4. **Low, fixed tonight. "Which letter is this?" asked the reverse:** the card
   names the letter and the tiles are shapes. Now "Which letter makes this
   sound?".
5. **Low. Odd options** (Q-095): a lone hamza as a letter, alif madda in a
   middle form, untaught letters as decoys in lesson 1.
6. **Low. Pressure after the first lesson** (Q-096): "Leave them and they
   fade."

## What went well for them

The "Do you already know some Urdu?" question fits them exactly; there is a
script-first track; letter cards show all four shapes with real words; a
careful trace was accepted; hearts are free in Unit 1; no sideways scroll at
320x568, "More below" showed, no page errors.

## What this is not evidence for

A script playing a persona is not a person, and whether they would quit is a
judgement. The answer bot guessed, getting 22 of 42 right, so the feel of the
feedback and the numbers would differ for a real learner. Sound was not
tested. Not covered: the Unit 1 review beyond its first question, Unit 2,
Letter Lab, Profile. Next persona: 2, a complete beginner.
