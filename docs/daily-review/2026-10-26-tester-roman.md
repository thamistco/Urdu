# 2026-10-26 · Tester, persona 3: a Roman-track learner

The third run of `qaaf-tester` (36 tool calls, about 18 minutes, read-only).
The persona is 28, from Leicester, knows a little spoken Urdu from their
in-laws, wants to speak and will not learn the script yet. Onboarded as they
would (Speak with family, Roman Urdu, the woman's voice, starting from
scratch, Steady), then played all of the first lesson at 390x844 and again at
320x568, then Home and Review.

## Where they would most likely give up

**Nowhere in what was covered.** The likeliest point is the first lesson's
length: "First words" is 45 steps for 11 words, and leaving it at step 30
starts it again from the beginning, which the dialog now says plainly (Q-114,
with Q-038).

## What confused, misled or slowed them, worst first

1. **The first screens are pitched at script learners:** "Speak Urdu? Now read
   it." then "Start with the alphabet"; "Learn the whole course in English
   letters instead" is plain text that does nothing (Q-103); the Roman choice
   comes two screens later.
2. **"Type this word"** under "Water" reads as "type Water"; the placeholder
   rescues it (Q-115).
3. **No answer for "knows a little"** among "new to me" and "I grew up around
   it" (Q-115).
4. **Script on a Roman Home:** the Letter Lab tile, "قاف" in the header, the
   Numbers node's ۳ (Q-086).
5. **Glosses:** haal "Present / condition" where a family speaker means "how
   you are"; some options carry an emoji and others none; the dotted ṭ in
   "beṭa" unexplained (Q-115).

## What went well for them

The Roman choice is clear when it comes, and holds: no Urdu script appeared
anywhere in the lesson at either size. Typing forgives spelling (pani, kitab,
nam, kam accepted). Wrong answers are gentle, hearts are free in Unit 1, the
results are honest, and Review showed what was due. At 320x568 everything
fitted.

## What this is not evidence for

A script playing a person, missing one answer in four on purpose; sound was
headless; lesson 2, the re-asks, a review session, Practice's Grammar and
Reading tabs, and Unit 10's readings (the likeliest place for script to leak
onto this track) were not reached. Next persona: 4, a parent choosing for a
child of 9.
