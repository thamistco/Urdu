# 2026-10-27 · Tester, persona 4: a parent choosing for a child of 9

The fourth run of `qaaf-tester` (34 tool calls, about 18 minutes, read-only),
on the ed0c764 build. A parent in Bradford tries lesson 1 for her 9-year-old:
"Read & write it", Script first, the woman's voice, "I already speak or
understand it", the quick check guessed. Then "Meet the letters" played as a
guessing child would, then Profile, League, the privacy and terms pages, and
lesson 1 at 320x568. The app made no request to any host but its own.

## Where the child would most likely give up

**Lesson 1, step 13 of 42, "Which tile is alif?"** By then the guessing child
has heard "Not quite" on 5 of 6 questions, with 29 steps and 5 re-asks to come;
the run ended at 13 of 31 (42%). The answer said "alif is the second tile from
the right" while the tiles sat two by two, where "from the right" means
nothing (Q-116). Forty-two steps is long for a first sitting at 9 (with Q-038,
Q-114).

## What confused, misled or slowed them, worst first

1. **"Understands spoken Urdu" skips most of the word lessons**, 50 of 81
   Beginner lessons, and the parent is never told how much (Q-118).
2. **Small, faint print** on the letter cards for a child; at 320x568 "More
   below" covers the Middle and End labels and Got it is below the fold
   (Q-119, with Q-016).
3. **"The answer: End."** when the buttons said "Middle or end". **Fixed
   tonight.**
4. **Gentle pressure:** "Free until Unit 1 is done" from step 1, "Leave them
   and they fade" on Home (**reworded tonight**, P-124), 15th of 15 in a
   League "cohort" a child will not understand (Q-120).
5. **The privacy page names no one to contact** (O-11, already the owner's).

## What went well for them

No real money anywhere; gems are earned and buy only hearts and freezes. Wrong
answers are "Not quite, but that’s okay". Tracing forgives a child's line
("Beautifully done" for a wobble beside alif: kind, if weak as teaching). The
words are child-safe. The privacy page has a plain Children section, and during
play nothing was sent anywhere.

## What this is not evidence for

A script playing a parent and a child, guessing on purpose; no sound, no
sign-in, and the out-of-hearts screen was not reached (the script stalled in
the Unit 1 review on a Check that stayed off; not confirmed as a bug). Next
persona: 5, a screen-reader user.
