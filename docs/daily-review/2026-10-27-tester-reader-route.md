# 2026-10-27 · Tester, route 1: a reader who skips the alphabet

The first learning-route run of `qaaf-tester` (22 tool calls, about 11 minutes,
read-only), on the ed0c764 build. The owner asked on 2026-10-26 for a route a
night beside the next persona, until every route is walked. A reader from
Lahore who already reads Urdu: "Read & write it", Script first, the woman's
voice, "I already speak or understand it", all four script questions right,
then "Skip the alphabet". The ready card said "You already read some Urdu" and
offered the skip correctly.

## Where they would most likely give up

**Straight after the skip.** Home's Continue opened Unit 1's review, 22
questions on what they had just been told was done (which letter makes this
sound, four times; which position, four times; tracing; paani, "No", "Woman").
A "Due for review" session followed, then Unit 2's review (16 of its first
prompts Unit 1's again). The first new lesson was Pronouns, in Unit 4, after
three reviews of skipped material and with five more before the next new
lessons. **Fixed tonight:** onboarding skips a unit's review when it skips
every lesson in that unit (Units 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 8 and 9 here).

## Other problems, worst first

1. **Hearts spent on skipped material:** after Unit 1's review, hearts were
   on for reviews of what was skipped. Fixed with the above: those reviews are
   now skipped too. The first real lesson is free and ends with the note that
   hearts are on, as finishing Unit 1 would.
2. **Home talks to them as a beginner:** "Read the script", "LVL 1
   Beginner", the Letter Lab card promoted, "Every letter has four faces. You
   will learn all of them." (Q-122).
3. **Two counts that do not agree:** Review shows every A1 topic known ("First
   Words 21/21 known") beside "Memory strength 0 of 22 mastered" (Q-123).
4. **"No wrong answers. This only works out where to start you."** A full
   score is what offers the skip, so the answers do matter (low).
5. **About 59 lessons show the skip mark**, which makes the real ones in A1
   hard to find (low).

No later letter lesson re-teaches what was skipped; letters still return
through due reviews, the Unit 9 script review and the Letter Lab.

## What went well for them

Onboarding is short and honest; the script questions are in the script; the
skip is a clear choice; answers explain themselves ("Same bowl as be, but
three dots below"); typing accepts Urdu input.

## What this is not evidence for

A bot cannot hear, so audio questions were guesses and its hearts lost say
nothing about this reader. Read faster was not reached; nothing from A2 up
was played; Unit 2's review opening on Unit 1 items is a lead, not confirmed.
Next routes: a speaker on Roman, testing out by a unit review, switching
track.
