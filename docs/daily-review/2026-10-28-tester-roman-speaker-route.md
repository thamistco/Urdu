# 2026-10-28 · Tester, route 2: a speaker on the Roman track

The second run of `qaaf-tester` on a learning route (30 tool calls, about 11
minutes, read-only), on the dd991087 build. The route: a speaker who wants to
talk with family and will not learn the script. In onboarding they chose
Speak with family, Roman Urdu and the man's voice, said "I already speak or
understand it", and got all four quick-check words right.

**No lesson was finished.** The answering script looped on a sentence missing
from its own word list. That fault is the script's, not the app's.

## Where they would most likely give up

**Right after the quick check.** The result read "Your starting level: You
already read some Urdu", to someone who had just declined the script, after a
check entirely in English letters. **Fixed tonight:** reading is judged only
where the check was in the script.

## Other problems, worst first

1. **Reviews and counts disagree.**
   - Profile shows 501 of 2,275 words known, and Practice topics show "21/21
     known".
   - Daily review says "Nothing to review yet".
   - The skipped words are known by ear by design (Q-093), so they never come
     due. Nothing says so (Q-137).
2. **Letters on a track with no alphabet:**
   - the Letter Lab card;
   - "0/40 Letters";
   - the ح avatar;
   - Unit 3's ۳ (Q-122, Q-086).
3. **Two START badges:** Unit 4's Pronouns, and Unit 10's "My best friend"
   (Q-138).
4. **"Both together" is pre-chosen as Recommended** on the track screen (low,
   by design).
5. **"Free this lesson"** shows with nothing to say why. Its end screen does.

## What the route was asked to check

**No Urdu script was found** in:

- Unit 10's readings;
- Practice Grammar and Reading;
- Review.

Read faster was not found on this track, as intended (not confirmed).

## What went well

- The quick check suits a speaker.
- "Moved ahead" explains the skips.
- Grammar is plain English with Roman examples.
- Readings stay in Roman.

## What this is not evidence for

- Nothing about reviews coming due, hearts across lessons, XP or achievements.
- A script with a word list is not a person who understands spoken Urdu.
