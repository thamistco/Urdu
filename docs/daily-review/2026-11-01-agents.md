## Q-019: how it went

The upgrade was built in its own worktree with its own `node_modules`, on a local branch, so the live branch and the agents' build were untouched.

- **The upgrade (83095fb8):** Expo 54, React Native 0.81, React 19, react-native-web 0.21, Reanimated 4, NativeWind 4.2. `expo install --check` is clean. Typecheck, lint and the 562 unit tests passed at once, after one JSX type fix.
- **React Navigation moved too.** The lockfile took @react-navigation/native from 7.3.13 to 7.5.1, core from 7.21.10 to 7.24.0, and elements to 2.9.45. The commit message does not say so (the second check, M1). It is recorded here for whoever bisects the next navigation bug.
- **First check:all failed at the licence notices.** npm now nests `@expo/cli` under `expo`, and the generator read packages only from the top of `node_modules`. The fix is in 93dfb892, and the notices were regenerated in 821594f2.
- **Second check:all failed at check:controls:** Settings and the League could not be opened. Tapping a tab changed the address, but the new tab stayed hidden under the old one. The app's own TabScene read `navigation.isFocused()` during render, and React Navigation 7.5 no longer re-renders the layout on a tab switch. Fixed in 5674bea5 with `useIsFocused()`. This was a blocking regression, and check:controls caught it, not the second check.
- **Third check:all failed once at check:stability, on the offline audio case.** It reported "Offline, tapping Hear on l-1 played nothing and said nothing". The step had passed on both earlier runs. It also passed on three reruns of the check alone, and 10 of 10 times on each build in a probe of that one case. It failed while the second check was driving browsers on the same machine. That load is the likely cause, but it was not shown. Recorded as a flaky check (Q-174). The check was not touched.
- **Fourth check:all:** passed before the push. The push and the live-site step are in the summary.
