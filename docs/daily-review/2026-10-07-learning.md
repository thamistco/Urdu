# 2026-10-07 · Learning effectiveness (area 4)

First review of this area. `qaaf-learning` generated every lesson with the
real generator (`buildLessonExercises` through `scripts/lib/load-ts`) and
measured from that, not from reading the code. The existing checks for
shape, coverage, order, answerability and spacing all pass.

## What it measured

- **Repetition is good.** Each of the 2,279 new words is met 4 or 5 times in
  its first lesson (mean 4.41): a choice question, the teaching card, recall
  from the meaning, then producing it, with matching for 41% of words. That is
  3.4 graded sightings in 3.4 different shapes.
- **After the first lesson it is all the scheduler's.** 77% of words (1,751)
  are not met again in the next five lessons on the path. Their second meeting
  depends on review, at 4 due items per teaching lesson.
- **`gauntlet/BENCHMARKS.md` is out of date:** it still says 1.3 minutes and
  1.8 sightings a word, where `check:shape` now measures 5.2 minutes and about
  4.4.
- **Spacing fits the research.** A miss returns in a minute; right answers go
  to 1 day, 3 days, then by an ease near 2.5 (about 7, 17, 41). The best gap is
  about a fifth of the time you want to remember for (Cepeda et al. 2008), and
  the total spacing matters more than whether the gaps grow (Karpicke and
  Bauernschmidt 2011).
- **The script order is sound:** nine letter lessons of four to seven letters,
  about seven sightings a letter, letters spotted inside real words, tracing,
  and look-alike drills; reading starts after the alphabet.

## Bug found and fixed tonight

**Words were built from letters not yet taught.** On the script and "both"
paths, 232 of 1,233 word-building exercises asked for letters the path had not
reached. The first was the second lesson of the course: زندگی after six
letters, five of them never taught. Building picks each letter out by its
shape, so this tested before teaching. A word is now built only once all its
letters are taught on that track's path, and typed or recalled otherwise.
Measured 232 to 0, leaving 999 builds; a unit test walks both paths and fails
with the rule removed. Commit 6382016.

## Proposals, not built tonight

1. **A word answered correctly before it is due keeps its interval (P-009,
   queued).** A unit's closing review fills with its own words when nothing is
   due, so a word taught and reviewed the same day jumps from 1 day to 3
   without an overnight gap: 243 of 243 in a simulation. Against: FSRS-style
   schedulers give early reviews partial credit, and a heritage speaker who
   already knows a word would move up more slowly. Precondition of the number:
   one sitting with every answer right, so it says nothing about real
   learners' recall.
2. **Fade the Roman on the "both" track once a word is known (P-010, queued).**
   The default track shows Roman under every word for good, so a learner can
   finish without decoding Nastaliq; transliteration helps as a scaffold that
   is taken away. Against: "both" promises Roman underneath, and some chose it
   for that comfort. A promise change, so it wants its own night and a check
   from a heritage learner's side.
3. **The first-sighting pretest is weaker evidence than its comment says
   (judgement).** Choosing among wrong answers did not beat simply reading in
   Potts and Shanks (2014); generating them did. No change proposed yet.
