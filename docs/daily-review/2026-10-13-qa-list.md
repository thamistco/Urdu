## Skipped, and why

- **H1, lesson length and resume.** Splitting lessons to 15–20 steps reshapes
  all 350 lessons and how finished lessons map to new ones. That is P-046 and
  needs its research and second check. Resuming a lesson partway (Q-038) is a
  feature of its own. The step counter shipped on 12 October, and the in-app
  dialog replaced `window.confirm` then too.
- **H2, grammar order.** Moving g-perfect and g-imperative earlier, or rewriting
  the early sentences, is a curriculum change (P-062). The suggested build
  check would fail on today's content, and a check that fails on arrival
  blocks every push.
- **H6, gender.** Needs feminine variants written for the first-person
  sentences, their audio, and a native check (P-048).
- **H7, contact on the legal pages.** Needs a real address and the data
  controller's name from the owner. Neither can be made up.
- **M5, a "because" on comprehension questions.** Needs one written per
  question, for 29 passages and dialogues.
- **M7, level tags.** The levels also choose decoys and sibling pools, so
  re-deriving them changes what the generator offers (Q-043).
- **M10, repeats per sentence.** The five touches per sentence are a
  deliberate climb, held by check:shape. Cutting them is how the lessons get
  shorter, so it goes with H1.
- **M11, longer reading and dialogue lessons.** Needs questions written
  (P-067).
- **M14, topic moves.** Moving words between topics regroups lessons that
  learners have already finished. The pantry topic was renamed for its spices
  on 12 October instead (Q-056).
- **M16, stricter tracing and stroke order.** Stricter coverage conflicts with
  the earlier finding that tracing with a mouse already fails, and stroke
  order needs stroke data for 40 letters in four forms (P-049).
- **M22, deep links answer 404.** The 404 status comes from GitHub Pages; the
  app still opens the screen. A redirect page needs a change to
  `.github/workflows/`, which this session cannot push (O-4).
- **M23, offline support.** A service worker (P-016) risks serving a stale app
  after a deploy and wants its own design.
- **M24, the rest.** Splitting content out of the main bundle, and reserving
  space to cut layout shift.
- **M25, words before their letters.** Curriculum order, and a teach-before-test
  check would fail on today's content.
- **M27, the rest.** The 200% text case goes with the 375–414 px sweep (Q-048).
- **L2, tabs and the SCRIPT section.** The section holds the Roman toggle and
  the learning track, so its name fits. Tab icons are a design pass.
- **L3, more achievements; L5, playback in Say it back.** Features (P-058,
  P-050).
- **L9, merging the two افق entries.** Merging ids touches saved progress and
  needs a retired-id mapping (Q-059).
- **L10, month romanisations.** Choosing one scheme wants a native or
  dictionary check (P-064).
- **L15, speaker labels.** Polish, not reached.
- **L19, a speaker on "How do you say it?" options.** It would answer the
  question (Q-061).
- **L6, "dermiyaan".** It is only an id, which no learner sees; renaming an id
  would orphan saved progress.
- **Audio re-recordings.** Held, as asked. The one clip generated was se-word,
  because L12 changed its word.
