## Decided tonight

- **Positioning stays "Speak Urdu? Now read it":** heritage literacy within a
  full course. For: Matra built the same pitch for Hindi, Bol goes the other
  way, Ling is a generalist. Against: BolNama targets the same buyer, and a
  heritage-only claim turns beginners away. **Never lead with "two voices":**
  BolNama advertises two native offline voices and Qaaf's are synthesised.
  The store copy already says this, so nothing changes; recorded so it is not
  reopened.
- **P-001, lifetime plan (input for its second check, not decided):** the
  agent suggests a lifetime unlock at launch at $179.99, 2.6x the yearly
  price, against a 3-5x rule of thumb ([pricepush](https://pricepush.app/glossary/lifetime-purchase))
  and because 35% of annual cancellations come in month 1 ([RevenueCat SOSA
  2026 via ppc.land](https://ppc.land/95-of-annual-app-subscribers-who-cancel-never-return-revenuecat-finds/)).
  Against: with no analytics (Q-005) nobody could see whether lifetime eats
  the yearly plan, and uTalk sells 150 languages for $70. P-001 waits for
  payments (Q-002), so this waits with it.
- **Q-019 (expo-av to expo-audio):** carry silent-mode playback over
  (`playsInSilentModeIOS` today in `src/lib/sound.ts` and
  `src/components/SayItBack.tsx`); Matra's audio complaints show how heritage
  apps lose ratings. Added to Q-019.
