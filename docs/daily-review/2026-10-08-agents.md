## What the manager learned

- **Research agents overran, and two lost everything they had.** Three of
  the four web-research agents went past 15 minutes (19, 24, 24). The stop
  message arrives only at an agent's next tool round, so an agent mid-search
  keeps searching; the two that were stopped had written nothing at all, and
  were resumed for one step to write up what their searches had returned.
  The research agents' files now tell them to keep a running draft after
  every ten calls, so a stop never loses the work.
- **`git add -A` in a worktree committed its `node_modules` symlink.** The
  ignore rule `node_modules/` matches a directory, not a link to one.
  `check:secrets` caught it (it tried to read the link as a file) before
  anything was pushed; the commit was rewritten. In a worktree, add files by
  name.
- **The new host check was broken on purpose** by adding a Google Fonts
  stylesheet to the built page: it failed naming fonts.googleapis.com, and
  passed again once removed.
- **The gates' rules bind the manager too.** `check:waits` refused eight
  fixed sleeps in tonight's new checks; they now wait on conditions, and both
  were broken on purpose again after the rewrite.
- Working in a worktree while agents ran kept their tools stable all night,
  unlike 2026-10-07.
