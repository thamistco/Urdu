# 2026-10-15 · Growth and referral, from the learners' side (area 13)

`qaaf-learner-voice`, 26 tool calls, 2.3 minutes, read-only. Duolingo's blog,
a Reddit mirror and one blog were blocked, so most of this comes from search
summaries. No Urdu-app store review mentioning sharing or family use was found.

## What learners share, and why

- **Funny sentences**, for years and widely: strange Duolingo prompts shared as
  a joke and a shared struggle, not as invitations ([KnowYourMeme](https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/sites/strange-duolingo-sentences)).
- **Pride:** Duolingo says thousands share streaks daily and that learners
  liked sharing percentile stats (its own blog, summary only).
- **Urdu:** couplets travel on WhatsApp ([Rekhta](https://rekhta.org/tags/whatsapp-shayari/ghazals));
  Rekhta's app sells sharing "with family" in Urdu, Hindi and Roman side by
  side. Product claims, not a survey.

## Family learning

No Urdu app for parents and children learning side by side was found; the
nearest are Kids Bolo (live classes) and children's qaida apps. Heritage
stories of learning to read with a grandmother are single accounts ([CAL](https://www.cal.org/heritage/pdfs/voices-urdu-language.pdf)).
A Stanford prototype (Hi! Langee) found parents used their heritage language
more often with gentle prompts (small diary study, [IJDL](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/journals/index.php/ijdl/article/view/42169)).
Comparing siblings makes the slower one shy or competitive (a vendor's
article).

## What backfires

Nagging ("an annoying nagging parent", a June 2026 spike of "creepy
notification" reviews); friend streaks and quests that create pressure or
break; losing a streak as the moment to quit; paid referrals gamed with fake
accounts. Each said repeatedly, one post at a time.

## Proposals (checked once)

- **P-021, sharpened:** share a word the learner can now read, Urdu then Roman
  then meaning, offered once at a reading milestone, never as a pop-up, linking
  to the first lesson (P-041). For: a family already passes Urdu round on
  WhatsApp; a word is pride without a number to compare; the Roman line keeps it
  readable. Against: nothing shows shares bring learners and Qaaf cannot
  measure it; WhatsApp shows Naskh, not Nastaliq; another button.
- **P-082** A written rule: no invite prompts, no rewards for sharing, no
  friend streaks, no comparing learners. For: what people who left other apps
  name; parents' worry about comparison. Against: Duolingo reports shared
  streaks make people 22% more likely to finish the day's lesson, so this gives
  up a working lever.
- **P-083** "Read it to someone": after the alphabet unit, an optional card,
  "Read these three words to someone who speaks Urdu". Nothing leaves the
  device. For: family who speak Urdu become the teachers; learning side by side
  with no accounts. Against: judgement only; may leave out learners with no
  Urdu speaker nearby, so it must be easy to skip.

## What this is not evidence for

How many people share, whether shares bring learners, or what Urdu families
on WhatsApp actually do.
