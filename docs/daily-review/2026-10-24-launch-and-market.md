No QAAF or QAF mark was found in classes 9, 41 or 42, and no "Qaaf" app on
Google Play. Desk research cannot settle it: search indexes only part of the
registers, and pending applications and unregistered UK rights (passing off)
are not covered. Only a clearance by a trademark attorney does.

**For keeping the name:** none of these teaches a language, and "Qaf" is a
common Arabic letter and the name of a sura, which makes exclusive rights weak
for everyone. **Against:** every hit sells to Muslim learners of Arabic and
the Quran, who overlap heavily with Urdu-speaking families, and Qaf, LLC is
funded, growing and may file.

**Decided:** Q-010 stays open, and its decision is the owner's (O-13): accept
the risk on desk research, or pay for a clearance. Changing a name is
cheapest before anything is publicised.

## Before a publicised launch: one thing missing from the bar

**Where Qaaf lives, for good.** Progress is kept in the browser per web
address, so moving from thamistco.github.io/Urdu to a domain later empties
every learner's progress, the same harm as renaming the storage keys, which
CLAUDE.md forbids. The address has to be chosen before any link is
publicised. A domain costs money, so this is the owner's (O-12), and the
launch bar gains it.

The owner's list before a publicised web launch, in order: the address
(O-12); the name (O-13); who holds the data, for the privacy page (O-11); sign
-in on the web working fully or hidden (O-6 or P-094); and where in-app
reports go (O-10 or P-108), for R7.

**Proposed, not decided:** always writing the name in full, "Qaaf: Learn
Urdu", in the stores and in posts, to set it apart from the Islamic AI apps.
Against: it weakens the short name.
