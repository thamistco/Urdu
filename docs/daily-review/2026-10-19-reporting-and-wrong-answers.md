# 2026-10-19 · Feedback (area 14) and what learners say (area 15): the report control, and the moment an answer is wrong

Researched by `qaaf-learner-voice` (26 tool calls, read-only). Five sites were
blocked (blog.duolingo.com, a Reddit mirror, community.wanikani.com,
alllanguageresources.com, forum.clozemaster.com); points from them come from
search summaries and are marked so. No Reddit thread text, app-store review or
r/Urdu post was found on either question.

## Area 14: where other apps put "report", and how it looks

- **Duolingo** puts a Report flag beside the solution after Check, and its
  menu differs for answers marked right and wrong, so people report from right
  answers too. 72% of reports are "My answer should have been accepted" and
  about 10% lead to a fix
  ([blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-user-reports-improve-course-content),
  summary; the company's own figures).
- **Clozemaster** has a red flag on the play screen. One user wants a list of
  their own reports and to see whether a sentence was already reported;
  another says "nothing seems to change" after flagging; a third wants the
  report button beside each sentence, not at the end of a round
  ([thread](https://forum.clozemaster.com/t/is-there-a-way-to-see-the-sentences-ive-flagged/19984),
  [thread](https://forum.clozemaster.com/t/buttons-after-round-of-cloze-listening-dont-seem-relevant/27885),
  summary). One user each.
- **Memrise:** a flag or three-dot menu on the card, from a third-party
  complaints site only; unverified.
- **Babbel and Anki:** no in-lesson button. Babbel asks for the lesson name by
  chat or email
  ([support](https://support.babbel.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360055163731-Sharing-feedback-with-Babbel));
  Anki's advice is a review on the shared deck's page.
- **Whether a bare flag is understood:** no learner evidence either way.

## Area 15: the moment an answer is marked wrong

- **People complain about being marked wrong unfairly, not about the tone:**
  a typo that is a real word, inconsistent typo tolerance, names, missing
  accents ([Well-Trained Mind](https://forums.welltrainedmind.com/topic/527027-are-we-the-only-ones-having-trouble-with-duolingo/),
  [Substack](https://mvanec.substack.com/p/duolingo-annoyances), r/duolingo
  via a mirror, summary). All of it is about Duolingo.
- **Not being shown the answer** is the only Urdu-specific point: a reviewer of
  the Aamozish app called it frustrating that it "won't tell you the right
  answer" (summary). Qaaf's answer card already shows it.
- **Hearing it again:** WaniKani users ask for the audio after a wrong answer
  too (summary). Qaaf's replay button already does this.
- **Wording:** a replication study found people learn less from "you were
  wrong" than from "you were right" feedback, and rewording it from
  self-focused to task-focused did not close the gap
  ([Gok and Fyfe 2022](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/68j7q1t4)). A thesis
  argues that naming the error reduces a learner's uncertainty
  ([Landzettel](https://gmwpublic.studenttheses.ub.rug.nl/5928/1/Final-Thesis-Timo-Landzettel.pdf)).
- **Quitting over one wrong answer:** no first-hand account found.

Judgement: the banner already gives what people ask for (the answer, the Roman
form, the audio). Nothing found says "Not quite, but that's okay" needs
changing. One gap: the answer card shows the right answer but not what the
learner wrote.

## What this changes

- **P-020** (held since 17 October for where "Report" goes on the banner)
  gains two points for its next attempt: a text label, "Something wrong?",
  rather than a bare flag; and the control on right answers too, offering only
  "The Urdu looks wrong" and "It sounds wrong" there. A quiet "Reported" on an
  item already reported is noted for later. **Against:** a link on the success
  banner clutters the reward moment and adds low-value reports when about 90%
  already lead nowhere; the flag is a convention Duolingo users know, so
  preferring words is judgement.
- **P-099 (new): "You wrote: …" above "The answer"** on a typed answer marked
  wrong, so the one letter that differed shows. For Roman-track learners and
  heritage speakers checking a spelling variant. **Against:** it sets a mistake
  beside the right form, adds height on small screens, and rests on indirect
  evidence (a thesis and Duolingo complaints).

Neither was built tonight: P-020 waits for the banner measurement from the
design review (`2026-10-19-agents.md`).

## What this is not evidence for

None of it comes from Qaaf's learners, Urdu learners on Reddit, parents, or
people who left an app. It says nothing on how often anyone reports from a
right answer, whether a flag icon is understood, or whether the banner's words
affect who comes back.
