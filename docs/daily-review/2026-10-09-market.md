`qaaf-growth` revisited the market three nights after the first pass
(`2026-10-06-market.md`), 27 tool calls, read-only. apps.apple.com, bolt.host

## What changed since 10-06

**Duolingo is no longer the reference point.** The heritage-script niche now
holds small apps that are free or sold once:

| App           | What it is                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Price                                            |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **BolNama**   | Early-access page only. Claims every letter in four forms, offline audio in two voices, tum/aap and gender toggles, in-law and chai-time lessons, for heritage adults who understand Urdu but cannot read it | None yet; not in any store                       |
| **Bol**       | iPhone. Roman Urdu only, "every word written the way it sounds"; Urdu and Hindi full, Persian and Arabic beta                                                                                                | Free, one purchase "unlocks everything, forever" |
| **Matra**     | Hindi for heritage speakers, tracing, levels; 5.0 from a handful of ratings; one complaint: silent with the ringer off                                                                                       | Free, no purchases                               |
| **Humzabaan** | iPad, from two brothers in India (October 2024): courses, a Hindi/Urdu/English dictionary, literature                                                                                                        | Free                                             |
| **BOLI**      | A 2026 pre-seed company with an AI "Nana" character; no store listing                                                                                                                                        | None yet                                         |
| **KidsBolo**  | A flashcard companion to paid classes                                                                                                                                                                        | Classes $125 per child per month                 |

**The generalists:** Ling has the script, Roman and native audio, 4.48 from
33,002 Play ratings (tracker), and recurring complaints of upsell, bugs, audio
that does not match the text, and one set of materials for every language.
Pimsleur is audio-first at about $14.95 a month and, by one guide, does not
teach reading. Mondly is about $9.99 a month and mostly tests recognition.
uTalk sells 2,500 words over 60 topics with native voices. UrduPod101 is called
weak in structure and beginner-heavy.

## Where Qaaf stands (judgement)

- **Better:** the only course that is Urdu alone, from the letters through
  grammar to conversation. Deeper than Ling; teaches the script, unlike Bol and
  Pimsleur.
- **Worse:** in no store, so no ratings against Ling's 33,000; synthesised
  voices where uTalk, Ling and BolNama advertise native ones; nothing for
  children.
- **The real threat** for the next launch window is Ling, because it is what a
  searcher finds: its blog ranks for "Urdu learning app" and claims the script.
  BolNama is the closest copy of Qaaf's pitch, but has not launched.

## Proposals

   Apple's 2.3.7 forbids other apps' names in metadata, and the claim is

2. **Revisit "no lifetime plan" at P-001's second check.** Bol sells a one-off

   free; heritage learners may resist a subscription. Against: the 10-08 reason
   stands, since a lifetime plan can take sales from the yearly plan and nothing
   can measure that yet. Recorded on P-001.
3. **Narrow Q-009:** a hands-on teardown of Ling Urdu and Bol first, BolNama
   once it launches; check Ling's audio-text mismatches and whether Qaaf's
   lessons can switch tum and aap. Installing is the review's; buying is the
   owner's.
