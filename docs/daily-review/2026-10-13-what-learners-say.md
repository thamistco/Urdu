## Hearts and lives: the most widely reported complaint

- Free Duolingo users get about two to four lessons a day under its Energy
  system; a petition ([change.org](https://www.change.org/p/duolingo-hearts-not-energy)),
  a writer who ran out in the third lesson with no mistakes ([Android
  Authority](https://androidauthority.com/quitting-duolingo-energy-system-3599842))
  and r/duolingo posts say so.
- Duolingo says beginners were twice as likely to run out mid-lesson under
  hearts ([blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-energy/)), its own figure.
- Losing "practice to earn hearts" angered free users ([Lingoly](https://lingoly.io/duolingo-practice-to-earn-hearts-greyed-out-fix/)).

Bearing on Qaaf: the refill is 10 gems (one lesson's pay) since this morning,
and a lesson opens on the out-of-hearts screen rather than walling a learner
mid-lesson. "Earn a heart by reviewing" is already P-061, held on 12 October
because practising would lose the learner's place in the stuck lesson; this
evidence is added to it, and it still wants Q-038 first.

## Retrying and typing

- Learners say Duolingo marks typos inconsistently; **invisible character
  differences** cause wrong marks in Yiddish and Navajo. Urdu has the same
  trap: an Arabic keyboard types ي ك ه, which look like ی ک ہ and are other
  characters. **Fixed tonight** (e9c7571): typed script is folded to the Urdu
  letters before comparing.
- Lingvist tells a typo from an error and asks for a retype ([help](https://lingvist.com/help/how-do-you-count-mistakes)).
  A Kent study found second attempts usually better but warns that is not
  proof of learning ([Kent](https://www.cs.kent.ac.uk/pubs/2008/2788/index.html)).
  No source on whether learners prefer a retry.

## Proposals

- **P-071** "Almost: try again" on a typed near miss, at no heart cost, in
  place of "Close enough ✓". For: typo complaints span several threads;
  heritage and Roman-script learners type on phone keyboards. Against:
  "Close enough" already accepts these and teaches the spelling in the same
  breath, so a retry adds a step and taps for the same outcome; fluent
  heritage learners may find it patronising. Checked once.
- Resume a lesson partway: the evidence is thin; stays Q-038, with the
  learning review's reason added (the retrieval already happened; only its
  record is thrown away).

## What this is not evidence for

Search summaries and single threads, not review counts. Nothing here measures
