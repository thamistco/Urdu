# 2026-10-17 · Growth and referral (area 13)

Researched by `qaaf-learner-voice` (22 tool calls, read-only). The r/Urdu
mirror failed, so Reddit points are from search summaries. No Urdu-app review
mentioning sharing, families or community was found.

**What Qaaf has:** no share, invite, partner or forum code. The one social
feature is a weekly league of invented names, labelled on screen "a practice
cohort, not real people" (`LeaderboardScreen.tsx:83`), with no way to hide it.

## What learners say

- **Sentence discussions were loved for the grammar help**, and missed years
  after Duolingo removed them ([r/duolingo](https://lr.psf.lt/r/duolingo/comments/1hh9hc2/does_anyone_here_like_duolingo));
  the cost was moderation ([Latino Rebels](https://latinorebels.com/2019/09/24/duolingolegal)).
- **Partner practice brings harassment**: women on HelloTalk and Tandem report
  unwanted romantic and sexual messages
  ([FluentU](https://www.fluentu.com/blog/hellotalk-review/),
  [Trustpilot](https://pl.trustpilot.com/review/tandem.net)).
- **Leaderboards cause pressure**: rank-checking late at night, botting to the
  top ([Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/my-language-learning-app-has-a-leaderboard-and-its-ruin-1837407062),
  [MetaFilter](https://www.metafilter.com/199369/I-Am-Going-Out-In-A-Blaze-Of-Glory)).
- **Friend streaks cut both ways**: a friend kept some going and made others
  feel guilty ([DesirabilityLab](https://desirabilitylab.com/backfill/backfill-2023-278-duolingo-social-features)).
- **Invites people resent** are those sent from their contacts without consent
  ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2012/08/03/users-claim-twoo-is-spamming-their-friends-social-network-says-its-just-not-clear-enough)).
- **Heritage learners**: relatives tease or criticise "even when they're being
  kind" ([NPR Life Kit](https://www.npr.org/2022/05/25/1101187823/how-to-learn-a-heritage-language));
  Urdu heritage learners speak well and read poorly
  ([UCLA NHLRC](https://nhlrc.ucla.edu/nhlrc/article/287883)).

## Proposals (first check)

- **P-083, reworded so a relative is not invited to correct:** once, on the
  completion screen after the alphabet unit, a card (not a pop-up), "Show
  someone what you can read now", with three words the learner has learned in
  Urdu and Roman; "Done" and "Not now". One boolean in `qaaf-progress`;
  nothing leaves the device. For: reading is the heritage learner's new
  strength, shown rather than tested (judgement on UCLA); a spouse's in-law
  becomes the encourager. Against: relatives may still laugh (NPR); some have
  no Urdu speaker nearby; no evidence it keeps anyone learning.
- **P-082 widened:** add no open chat, partner matching, public comments or
  contact access (no moderators); and a Settings switch, "Show the weekly
  league", on by default. Against: discussions were valued for exactly the
  grammar help given up; the league already says it is not real people; a
  setting nobody has asked for.

## What this is not evidence for

How many feel any of this; whether sharing or family prompts bring in or keep
learners; what Urdu families in particular do; whether Qaaf's league causes
pressure.
