# 2026-10-16 · What learners say (area 15)

Researched by `qaaf-learner-voice` (29 tool calls, about 2 minutes,
read-only). Five mirrors failed to resolve, so every Reddit and Blind point
comes from search summaries, not the threads. No app-store review of an Urdu

## Themes

1. **Learning to talk to family.** An American married into a Pakistani
   family FaceTimes her mother-in-law through her husband and "can always sort
   of feel the disconnect"; a heritage speaker replied to focus "much more on
   grammar than vocabulary" (r/Urdu, February 2026, snapshot). A heritage
   speaker whose mother "never corrects them"
   ([Blind](https://www.teamblind.com/post/would-like-to-get-better-with-my-hindiurdu-need-help-zjogk8go)).
   A tutor whose students learn for in-laws and grandparents
   ([Preply](https://preply.com/id/guru/8528321)).
2. **What made people give up: letter shapes and gendered address.** A Tes
   writer "was put off learning Urdu immediately" by a gendered-address rule
   and by "each letter has three distinct forms"
   ([Tes](https://tes.com/magazine/archive/nice-urdu-shame-i-failed-exam)). A
   Leeds Beckett paper asks for everyday vocabulary and script teaching that
   does not centre the four forms
   ([paper](https://eprints.leedsbeckett.ac.uk/id/eprint/3117/1/My%20paper.pdf)).
3. **Nastaliq is hard to decipher** for a reader of Naskh (r/Urdu, March
   2025); the advice was to switch fonts on a known sentence, which is P-023.
4. **Roman Urdu has no standard spelling**: عام and آم are both "aam"
   ([CLE](https://www.cle.org.pk/clt09/download/ahmed_translit.pdf)). Academic,
   not learners.
5. Duolingo still has no Urdu course for English speakers.

## Measured tonight: Roman spelling variants (proposal C)

Probed against the real matcher: mein, men (for main), hay, he (for hai), ap,
acha, kaisay, kese, khana and pani are accepted outright. nahi for nahiñ,
shukria for shukriya and kia for kya are accepted as "close enough" (one
letter off, and no other word). "mai" for میں is refused: its skeleton is two
letters, under the near-miss floor of three. Not loosened tonight: dropping a
final nasal would also accept hai for ہیں, which is a grammar error. Added to
P-064.

## Proposals (first check)

- **P-086, a short "talking to family" lesson**: lines for a call with an
  in-law or grandparent. For: spouses' stated goal; parents neutral.
  **Against:** one 2026 thread and a tutor page; Unit 1 already greets; an
  extra lesson delays script for script-first learners. Heritage speakers want
  grammar more than phrases.
- **P-087, the aap/tum note brought forward to the family unit** as a note,
  not a drill. For: beginners (the Tes writer quit over such a rule) and
  spouses (the wrong address to an elder is the mistake they fear). Against:
  one columnist, and early rules may be the overload that drove her off;
  costs heritage speakers a screen. Joins P-069.

## What this is not evidence for

How common any complaint is: each source is one post, column or paper, read
through summaries. Nothing from Qaaf's learners, from parents, or from people
who left an app.
