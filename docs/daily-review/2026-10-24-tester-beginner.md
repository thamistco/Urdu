The second run of `qaaf-tester` (34 tool calls, about 12 minutes, read-only)
on the 0736a24 build. The persona is 34, from Manchester, with no Urdu and no
Arabic script, learning to talk with a partner's family. It chose "Both
together", already selected, "Recommended" and "Best if you are not sure",
played at 390x844, then came back a day later at 320x568.

## Where they would most likely give up

**In the middle of the first lesson,** "Meet the letters", 42 steps on an "8
minutes a day" goal. The first card says the forms are "joined only from the
right" before anything has said that Urdu runs right to left; then come many
"Which position is this letter showing?" and "Which letter makes this sound?"
questions whose wrong choices are letters never seen (Q-095); at 13 of 42,
"Which tile is alif?" shows four pieces of a word with one letter in orange
and nothing says the orange letter is the point or that the first tile is on
the right. Guessing as a beginner would, the run made 17 mistakes. Each is
answered kindly, but it is a lot of "Not quite" for a first lesson (Q-102).

   mostly alif-like letters a straight line really does trace; `check:trace`
   now plays them (Q-097).
2. **Low to medium. A review is due straight after lesson 1:** "4 things to
   bring back · Leave them and they fade", and 6 the next day (Q-096).
3. **Unconfirmed. Continue on the goal screen twice did not advance** by role
   or by its centre; tapping the word always worked. Possibly the harness

4. **Low.** "Learn the whole course in English letters instead" under "Let's
   start" looks like an option and does nothing; a reload partway through

   explained (Q-103); "Speak with family" is subtitled "Parents, grandparents,

## What went well for them

Onboarding is short and has "I'm starting from scratch"; mistakes are
answered kindly, with the answer and a reminder ("Alif wearing a wavy hat");
hearts are free in Unit 1; at 320x568 the next day's Home and its review had
no sideways scroll and every button was reachable; the streak message is not
scolding; no page errors.

## What this is not evidence for

A script playing a person, answering at random: the 57% says nothing about how
hard the questions are. Sound was off. Not covered: "First words", or Home
after more than a day. Next persona: 3, Roman track.
