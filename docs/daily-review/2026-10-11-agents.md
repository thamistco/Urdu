## Bugs found and fixed tonight

1. **Every lesson opened stayed in memory** (914b96a), from the performance
   review: about 6 MB a lesson, with no ceiling. See
   `2026-10-11-performance.md`. Measured before and after; a check now fails
   if a lesson is left mounted.
2. **Native sign-in would always have failed** (6f6201f): the Supabase client
   used the implicit flow, and native sign-in waits for a PKCE code. Found by
   P-036's second check, from reading the code; there is no native build to run
   it on yet.
3. **The speaker's quick check contradicted itself** (a59d73c): "I can't read
   the script", then a quiz in the script (Q-032).
4. **Content** (28ca0c1): the rest of Q-022 and the sample's errors, one word
   recorded again in both voices.
5. **Small things learners meet:** the typing box took taps in only a strip of
   itself (cbd39ee); a first meeting with a word did not say it was a guess
   (9f76107); "Leave this lesson?" appeared after only reading a teaching card
   (1f556c0); the Review tab's meter counted letters the Roman track cannot
   show (d7c173f).

QA confirmed last night's five changes: review first at 31 due and not 30, a
catch-up played to Finish; the Roman track with only letters due; the welcome
back shown, kept after an abandoned lesson and gone after a finished one; the

## What the manager learned

- **A probe can pass for the wrong reason, and a check can click the wrong
  thing.** The rewritten leave-lesson check first picked the close button as
  its "answer"; it now names what it must not click.
- **A workaround hid a leak.** Last night's leave-lesson check looked for the
  visible close button because a lesson left by its URL stayed mounted under
  Home; that was the leak, which tonight's performance run measured and traced
  to its cause.
- **Research agents finish in about two minutes** when the question is narrow
  and their sources are reachable; the drivers take their whole budget.
