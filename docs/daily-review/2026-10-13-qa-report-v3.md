## Not changed, by design or by decision

- **"Eight dialogue lines are byte-identical in both voices."** By design: a
  dialogue's two speakers have fixed voices (`voices: { A: 'f', B: 'm' }`), so
  both voice sets hold the same recording of each line.
- **M15, a listening card after the pretest costs a heart.** That is the
  hearts rule. Only pretests and traces are exempt.
- **H8, unit reviews fill from the due queue first.** The B2 letter-first part
  was fixed on 12 October. Reserving most of a review for its own unit trades
  against the guarantee that slipping words come back first (`check:srs`), so
  it is a proposal: P-070.
- **"kurra-e-ardh" for kurra-e-arz.** ض written dh is a convention from Arabic
  romanisation, and dh is also the aspirated د. Folding them would merge real
  pairs, so it is left.
