# 2026-10-18 · Launch readiness (area 11)

Researched by `qaaf-growth` (29 tool calls, read-only). Google's help pages
would not load, so Play rules come from search summaries and third-party
guides.

## A web launch on World Urdu Day, 9 November (★ blocks it)

1. ★ **The privacy page does not yet meet UK GDPR article 13.** It lacks who
   holds the data and how to reach them, the legal basis for each use, how
   long data is kept, and the right to complain to the ICO
   ([Muckle](<https://www.muckle-llp.com/files/pdf/fa-privacy-notices-required-under-the-uk-gdpr(1).pdf>)).
   Who holds the data is the owner's to give (O-11).
2. ★ **O-6 by about 5 November, or the web hides sign-in.** Otherwise Delete
   account says deletion is not switched on yet.
3. **A storage line, not a cookie banner.** UK storage law (PECR reg. 6)
   covers localStorage, but storage strictly needed for a service the user
   asked for, remembering their choices included, needs no consent
   ([ICO](https://ico.org.uk/about-the-ico/media-centre/news-and-blogs/2025/09/fact-vs-fiction-ico-debunks-myths-on-storage-and-access-technologies)).
   Judgement: Qaaf's keys qualify.
4. **Children:** the page covers under-13s (the US framing; COPPA's amended
   rule applied from 22 April 2026). The UK Children's code covers under-18s
   wherever children more probably than not use a service
   ([Orrick](https://orrick.com/Insights/2021/07/The-UKs-Age-Appropriate-Design-Code-Comes-into-Force-in-September-2021));
   judgement: a family app probably meets that test.
5. **An imprint is probably not needed**: Germany's applies to providers based
   there; the UK e-commerce rule asks for a name, address and email, and
   whether a free service is covered is unconfirmed. Both depend on where the
   owner is based.

## A Google Play closed test

O-5 (account), Q-019 (Expo SDK 54, which targets API 36; earliest about 1
November, judgement), an EAS build tested on a phone with O-2, the console
forms (O-8: data safety, target audience, content rating, deletion URL),
store graphics (a 1024x500 feature graphic, 2 to 8 screenshots, a 512 icon),
O-7 (12 testers for 14 days running), then about a week to apply for
production. Judgement: a closed test about 6 November at the earliest,
production late November.

`docs/store-listing.md` still lacks the data safety answers (only in the
2026-10-08 notes), the target audience, the content rating, the deletion URL,
any graphics, and the name search.

## Proposals (first check)

- **P-093, the privacy page to article 13**, with the storage line, once the
  owner names who holds the data. Against: the name becomes public, and
  wording without a lawyer may be wrong.
- **P-094, hide sign-in on the web from 5 November unless O-6 is done.**
  Against: sign-in is the only cure for Safari's 7-day wipe (Q-069).
- **P-095, declare the Play audience "13 and over"**, with a short
  Children's-code note. Against: it narrows the family market (Q-003).

## What this is not evidence for

No lawyer read any of this. Whether sign-in is live on the site, and where
the owner is based, were not checked. The dates are estimates.
